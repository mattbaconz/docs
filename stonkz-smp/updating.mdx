---
title: 'Updating'
description: 'Update a running Stonkz SMP server from 1.0 to 1.0.1 without touching your configs or player data.'
---

Stonkz SMP 1.0.1 changes one plugin jar. You swap that file, and your configs, market and player data stay as they are.

## 1.0.1

**Fixed:** stock graph maps (`/stonkz map <item>`) no longer cut off their Low and High prices once an item costs $100 or more. The setup now ships **Stonkz 1.4.45**.

That is the only change. Every other file in 1.0.1 is the same as in 1.0.

### Update from 1.0

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stop the server">
    Stop your server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the old jar">
    Delete `plugins/Stonkz-1.4.44.jar`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the new jar">
    Copy `Stonkz-1.4.45.jar` from `server/plugins/` in the new download into your server's `plugins/` folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the server">
    Start it again. The console shows `Enabling Stonkz v1.4.45`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Do not copy the whole new `server` folder over a running setup. That would overwrite your configs, spawn and any changes you made. Swap only the jar.
</Warning>

Your Stonkz licence key stays in `plugins/Stonkz/config.yml`, so you do not need a new key.

## Fresh installs

If you have not installed Stonkz SMP yet, use the newest download and follow [Installation](/stonkz-smp/installation). It already has every update.
