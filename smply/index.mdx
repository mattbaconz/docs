**Smply** is the preset you apply on a fresh Lifesteal or Economy SMP so players get a finished hub before you ping Discord.

Apply a preset, set spawn, open `/hub`. Hearts, combat, sell, travel, and HUD share one GUI language instead of five plugins that do not.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/smply/hub-hover.gif" alt="Smply player hub with kits and warps" />
</Frame>

## Compatibility

Smply targets **Paper and Folia `1.20.6` through `26.1.2`** and requires **Java 21**.

Folia uses the same region-safe scheduler as Paper. That is scheduler support on those versions, not a claim for every Folia fork or untested future build (including 26.2).

<Note>
Paid builds require a license key in `config.yml`. Claim product id `SMPLY` in Discord, then paste the key before you go live.
</Note>

## Five-minute path

```text
/smply preset lifesteal
/setspawn
/hub
/smply doctor
```

First boot is **Vanilla+** (travel, rewards, HUD). Pick Lifesteal or Economy when you know the niche.

## Quick commands

```text
/hub
/heart
/sell
/auction
/shop
/quest
/smply doctor
```
