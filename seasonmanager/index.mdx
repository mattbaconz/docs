---
title: 'SeasonManager'
description: 'Season timers, grace periods and a cross-season stash for Paper servers.'
---

SeasonManager runs your season clock, announces milestones and controls the grace period. Players see the countdown in a bossbar; PlaceholderAPI exposes it to your existing chat, tab or scoreboard plugin.

**Core** is the free edition. **PRIME** adds the cross-season stash, timelines, world archives, Discord integration and a web dashboard. Install one edition per server.

Start with [Installation](/seasonmanager/installation), choose your [configuration](/seasonmanager/configuration), then run `/season start`. `/season info` shows the current state.

Use Paper or Purpur. The released editions are not supported on Folia or plain Spigot. See [Features](/seasonmanager/features) for the edition differences and current limits.
